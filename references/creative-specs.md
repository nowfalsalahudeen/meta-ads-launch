# Creative specs and rules

## Shapes to make for every ad

| Name | Size | Where it appears |
|---|---|---|
| feed | 1080 x 1350 (4:5) | Instagram and Facebook feed (upload this first) |
| square | 1080 x 1080 (1:1) | square placements and fallbacks |
| story | 1080 x 1920 (9:16) | Stories and Reels |
| link | 1200 x 628 (1.91:1) | link ads, right column, X, LinkedIn |

Upload feed as the main image, then **Replace** the square, vertical and horizontal crops with their own files. Never let one image stretch to all shapes.

Story and Reels cover the top and bottom of the screen with the platform's own interface: keep anything that must be read between about 13% and 80% of the height and away from the right edge of the lower half.

Page assets: profile picture 1080 x 1080 (shown as a circle, keep the mark in the middle 60%); Facebook cover 1640 x 924 (desktop shows the middle 820 x 312 ratio, phones show 16:9, so keep text and logo in the middle band).

## What goes on an ad

1. **Hook headline** (2 lines, large): a pain or a promise, not a feature name. Examples: "Stop typing invoices by hand", "Manage your whole business with ease", "Know who paid. Instantly."
2. **Badge**: the offer in two or three words ("FREE EARLY ACCESS").
3. **One button**: the action and the address ("Join free - example.com").
4. **Proof of the product**: the real screen in a browser frame and on a phone, in the market's language and currency. Never an invented screen.
5. **Two floating cards** showing the product doing its job (an action done, a confirmation sent) **or one feature checklist** of six things the product does. Run both kinds, so the ads do not all lean on one feature.

Dark navy variant for the lead ads (it stands out in a bright feed); light variant for the rest.

## Copy rules

- Say what the product does today. No prices before paid plans exist. No "available on Google Play" before it is.
- No invented numbers, testimonials or guarantees. No before/after claims.
- Primary text: one short paragraph, the pain, the answer, the free offer, "no card needed". Headline: the outcome in five words. Description: three words.
- Other languages: write each natively (not a translation of the first), keep the brand and address in Latin letters, set `"dir": "rtl"` for right-to-left languages, and have a native speaker read it.
- Do not mention protected characteristics or target by them.

## Screens used in the creatives

Capture the real app with realistic invented data in the target currency and language. Avoid greetings or names that read as another market's if the audience differs. The in-app floating "feedback" or debug buttons will appear in captures: that is acceptable on web, but hide them for store listings.

## Things that look good but break the ad

- Meta's AI image generation and enhancements (they invent people and alter screens): off.
- Multi-advertiser ads (it crops): off.
- Text in the outer 10% of the image: may be cut by a placement.
- Small text inside the screenshot: it will not be read; make the cards and headline carry the message.
