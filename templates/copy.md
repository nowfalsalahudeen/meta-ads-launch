# Copy patterns

Fill in the brackets. Say only what the product does today. Keep the offer true.

## Formula
**Pain or promise -> what the product does -> the offer -> "no card needed" (only if true).**

## Tracking link
`https://example.com/?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=test1&utm_content=<ad-id>`
(In Ads Manager paste the part after `?` into Tracking -> URL parameters.)

## Pain-led ad
- **Primary text:** Still [doing the painful thing]? [Product] [does the fix in one line]. [Offer, e.g. Free for up to N <things> during early access.] [No card needed.]
- **Headline:** [Outcome in about five words]
- **Description:** [Three words]
- **Button:** Sign up (or Learn more)

Example (fictional): "Still typing invoices by hand? Acme Invoices builds, sends and tracks them in one tap. Free for 5 invoices a month during early access. No card needed." / Headline: "Send your first invoice free" / Description: "Invoicing for freelancers".

## Feature-led ad
- **Primary text:** [Product] in one place: [feature 1], [feature 2], [feature 3], [feature 4] and [feature 5]. [Offer.] [No card needed.]
- **Headline:** [What you get, plainly]
- **Description:** [Offer in three words]
- **Button:** Sign up

## Second language
Write each language natively, not as a translation of the first, keep the product name and address in Latin letters, and have a native speaker read it. For right-to-left languages set `"dir": "rtl"` on the language in the config.

## Instagram bio
[What it does for whom in one line]. [Two or three verbs]. [Offer].

## Facebook page "About"
[Product] helps [who] [do what] in one place. [Offer].

## Sizing the first test
One ad set per language x about USD 10 a day for 2 days, one to three ads. Compare ads on link click-through rate and cost per click after 48 hours; keep the best one or two for 5-7 days. Do not optimise for sign-ups before roughly 50 sign-up events in a week.
